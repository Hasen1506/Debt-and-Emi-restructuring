THE REAL RATE · ONE PER LOAN

The rate on your statement isn't what a loan costs you.

Interest is added every month, so it compounds. On an Indian card, 18% GST is charged on that interest. A home or education loan can earn a tax break. RightDue counts all of it, gives every loan one rate a year, and ranks your loans by it.

42.00% 60.31%

A credit card at 42% a year, in India.

WHY 60.31%?

Rate on your statement42.00%
+ Interest added monthly, so it compounds+9.11%
+ GST on the interest (18%; RBI bars interest on GST)+9.20%
= What it costs you a year60.31%

The same breakdown opens from every loan in the app. A home loan at 8.5% costs 8.84%; a personal loan at 14% costs 14.93%.

HOW IT WORKS · A FEW MINUTES

Add a loan or card

Balance, rate and EMI from your statement. Type 25L or 1.5cr; the app reads it. Not sure of a number? Say so, and it starts from an estimate you can fix later. Your plan shows after the first loan; add the rest from Plan.

Say what comes in and goes out

Take-home pay and spending. The plan works out what's left each month for extra payments.

Pay, mark it, repeat

Home says what to pay this month. Mark each one paid; the plan moves on from what really happened.

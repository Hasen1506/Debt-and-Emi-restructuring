Pushing it up, or rolling it down —
a Greek god on every tab.

A small mythic identity for each payoff strategy. Avalanche is Sisyphus straining uphill in deep red. Snowball is a robed figure letting the boulder gather on its way down. Hybrid stands on the ridge with one hand on each. The mark scales from a triptych hero down to a forty-pixel medallion in the corner of a tab.

04 · Notes for engineering / brand

Why this works, briefly.

PALETTE

Red is reserved.

The deep terracotta red (#b8261c) is the Avalanche signal — used nowhere else in the product. The cream (#f4d9b7) is the figure color. Snowball borrows the existing ink and a pale ice blue; Hybrid is a literal split of the two.

SCALING

One mark, four scales.

The persona art is authored once at 480×320 (the hero triptych), reused at 240×38 in the banner strip, 40×40 in the medallion, and 24×24 as a glyph. Every scale uses the same SVG <symbol>, so the visual identity is consistent across surfaces.

RECOMMENDATION

Pair D + the Profile section.

The medallion (Direction D) gives the most personality in the corner without dominating the header. Keep the full triptych for Profile — it's the right place to let the user really consider the choice.
